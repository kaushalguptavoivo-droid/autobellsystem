# User Guide

## Getting Started
Once installed and running, the app sits quietly in your system tray
(bottom-right corner near the clock/Wi-Fi icon — look for the bell icon).
Right-click it to access Dashboard, Pause/Resume, Test Bell, and Exit.

## Scheduling Bells
1. Open **Dashboard** from the tray menu (or double-click the tray icon).
2. Go to the **Schedules** tab.
3. Click **+ Add Bell Period**.
4. Fill in:
   - **Period Name** — e.g. "Period 1", "Assembly", "Lunch".
   - **Time** — hour and minute (24-hour).
   - **Days** — "Every day" or pick specific weekdays.
   - **Bell Pattern** — Regular Period (1 ring), Assembly (3 long rings),
     Lunch (2 rings), or Custom (choose your own ring count).
   - **Sound File (optional)** — click Browse to pick your own .wav/.mp3,
     or leave it empty to use the built-in bell tone (works offline).
5. Click **🔔 Preview** to hear it before saving.
6. Click **Save**.

To change or remove a period, select it in the list and click
**Edit Selected** or **Delete Selected**.

## Bluetooth Speaker
1. Pair your speaker once in Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices
   (this app does not do the pairing itself — it uses whatever Windows
   already has paired and connected).
2. In the Dashboard, go to **Bluetooth & Sound**, pick your speaker from
   the dropdown, and click **Set as Bell Speaker**.
3. From then on, bells are sent directly to that speaker whenever it's
   connected. If it's ever off or out of range, bells fall back to your
   PC's normal speakers automatically.

## Pausing Bells
Use **Pause Schedule** from the tray menu or the Dashboard to silence all
bells temporarily (e.g. during exams or holidays) without deleting your
schedule. Click again to resume.

## Works Offline
Everything — scheduling, timing, and playing bells — runs entirely on
the PC itself. No internet connection is required at any point.
